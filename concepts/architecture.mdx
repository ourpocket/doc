---
title: "Architecture"
description: "How Sandbox simulation and stateless provider calls are separated."
---

Sandbox uses PostgreSQL for simulated payments, fiat wallets, balances, and transfers. It does not call providers or move real money.

Production reads an encrypted, validated project connection and makes a direct request to the selected provider. The response returns to the caller and is discarded: no provider transaction, balance, virtual account, recipient, payout, customer detail, webhook payload, or overview snapshot is persisted by OurPocket.

Applications configure and receive Paystack, Flutterwave, and Mono webhooks directly. They retain provider references and results for support, verification, and reconciliation.
