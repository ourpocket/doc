---
title: "Resource model"
description: "Understand the boundary between simulated Sandbox data and live provider operations."
---

A **Project** has separate Sandbox and Production environments. A **Provider connection** contains encrypted credentials for the project’s own Paystack, Flutterwave, or Mono account.

Sandbox has simulated payments, refunds, fiat wallets, balances, and transfers. Those resources are project and environment scoped and can be inspected through the Sandbox APIs.

Production is a transparent provider layer. Checkout, verification, refunds, payouts, beneficiaries, dedicated or virtual accounts, and provider activity are requested from the connected provider and returned to the caller. OurPocket does not retain the provider response, provider customer data, transaction data, balance, account data, webhook payload, or an activity snapshot.

Pass a transient `contact` only when the provider needs it. Keep provider references and returned results in your own system for later verification, reconciliation, and support. Provider webhooks are configured directly with each provider.
