---
title: "Resource model"
description: "Understand customers, operations, provider references, and events."
---

A **Project** represents your application. An **Environment** is its isolated Sandbox or Production context. A **Provider** is an external service; an **Integration** is that project's connection to a provider.

A **Customer** is a normalized end-user record. A **Payment** is a simulated charge or hosted checkout. A **Refund** belongs to a payment. A **Wallet** is a Sandbox fiat balance or a Production chain address. A **Transfer** is simulated movement between Sandbox wallets. **Transactions** are the read-only combined list of payments, refunds, and transfers.

Every financial resource has a UUID, project/environment, kind, status, request ID, timestamps, optional amount/currency, optional provider and provider reference, and operation-specific `details`. `pending` and `unknown` are not settled. Production wallets have no fiat balance. A routed payment stores its provider decision in `details.routingDecision`.

An **Event** records a state change; a **Webhook delivery** is an attempt to send that event to your endpoint. Events and deliveries have separate IDs. Delivery replay creates a new delivery for the same event. Use [transactions and inspection](/features/transactions) and [webhooks](/user-guide/webhooks) to trace an operation.
