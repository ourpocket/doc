---
title: "Accounts and projects"
description: "Organize application environments and management access."
---

Sign up, verify your email, and create a project in the dashboard. A project represents one application and has isolated Sandbox and Production data. The initial `op_test_sk_` key appears once in the project creation response. The dashboard's project and environment selectors scope resource, provider, webhook, and log views.

Management requests use your user JWT. Runtime operations use a project key. Generate and rotate keys under **API keys**; connect customer-owned Production accounts under **Providers**. See [authentication](/concepts/authentication), [API keys](/developer-guide/api-keys), and [going live](/guides/migrate-to-live).
