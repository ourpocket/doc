---
title: "Provider webhooks"
description: "Configure Paystack, Flutterwave, and Mono webhooks directly."
---

Configure your webhook URL directly in the Paystack, Flutterwave, or Mono dashboard. Verify each provider’s signature against its raw request body using the secret stored in your own application.

OurPocket does not relay provider webhooks, store their payloads, retry delivery, or replay provider events. When you receive a provider event, retain the provider reference and run your own verification or reconciliation workflow against that provider.

OurPocket connections may require a callback URL and webhook secret to validate a provider account, but provider event data remains outside OurPocket.
