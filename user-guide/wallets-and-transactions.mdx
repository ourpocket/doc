---
title: "Wallets and transactions in the dashboard"
description: "Inspect environment-scoped wallets, payments, and transfers."
---

Select a project and environment first. **Wallets** creates Sandbox fiat wallets or Production Turnkey/Privy chain wallets. Sandbox funding, debit, and transfer controls simulate money movement. Production chain wallets show a provider address, with no fiat currency or balance.

**Payments** starts and inspects payments and refunds. **Transactions** shows a read-only history of payments, refunds, and Sandbox transfers. Open a resource for its status, provider reference, request ID, and details. Use **API Logs** and **Webhooks** to follow the same operation into events and delivery attempts. See [wallets](/features/wallets) and [transactions](/features/transactions).
