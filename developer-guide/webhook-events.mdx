---
title: "Provider webhook events"
description: "Verify provider events in your own webhook endpoint."
---

Create webhook endpoints in the Paystack, Flutterwave, or Mono dashboard. Verify signatures using each provider’s documented raw-body procedure and secrets managed by your application.

OurPocket does not receive, relay, retain, retry, or replay provider webhooks. Store the provider reference and event outcome in your own system, then verify against the provider when needed.
