OurPocket exposes normalized [payments and refunds](/features/payments), [wallets and Sandbox transfers](/features/wallets), [events and signed webhooks](/user-guide/webhooks), [transactions and logs](/features/transactions), and [auditable payment routing](/features/routing).

Sandbox simulates financial activity without provider credentials. Production executes hosted checkout through Paystack or Flutterwave and chain wallet creation through Turnkey or Privy. [Provider capabilities](/user-guide/providers) determine which operations are available. See [current boundaries](/operations/current-limitations) before designing a Production wallet or ledger workflow.
