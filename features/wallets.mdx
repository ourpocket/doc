---
title: "Wallets and virtual accounts"
description: "Use simulated Sandbox wallets or provider-owned receiving accounts."
---

Sandbox fiat wallets are test-only, PostgreSQL-backed balances. Funding, debit, and transfers require an `Idempotency-Key` and never call an external provider.

Production has no OurPocket wallet or custody balance. “Wallets” in provider operations means Paystack dedicated accounts or Flutterwave virtual accounts owned by your connected provider account. Create and inspect them through `/v1/providers/paystack/virtual-accounts/*` or `/v1/providers/flutterwave/virtual-accounts/*`. Include transient contact data only where the provider requires it.

Account details are returned directly and discarded. Store the account reference and any details your business needs in your own system.
