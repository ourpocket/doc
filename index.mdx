Connect your financial providers once. Build against one consistent API.

OurPocket gives your application normalized payments, wallets, events, and operational controls. You keep your own provider accounts. OurPocket handles supported provider-specific checkout and wallet creation calls underneath.

## How it works

```text
Your application → OurPocket API → Paystack or Flutterwave hosted checkout
                                 → Turnkey or Privy chain wallet creation
                  OurPocket events → Your signed webhook endpoint
```

Sandbox is a financial simulator scoped to your project. Production payments use connected Paystack or Flutterwave accounts. Production chain wallet creation uses connected Turnkey or Privy accounts. [Capabilities vary by provider](/user-guide/providers); a catalog entry alone does not mean it can execute an operation.

Start with [the quickstart](/quickstart), then use [routing decisions](/features/routing), [events and webhooks](/user-guide/webhooks), and [API logs](/features/transactions) to inspect what happened.
